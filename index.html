Rough estimate: gross wall area is traced facade length × the CSV ceiling height. Gross window area includes single- and double-glazed windows; double-glazed area is a subset. Glazing defaults to FLOOR ATTRIBUTES and a window-level WALL ATTRIBUTES value overrides that default. Each DXF opening is matched once to a CSV opening in the same room and category (window or door); DXF marker suffixes are not treated as CSV wall numbers. Confirm the facade lines and opening areas before relying on totals. Interior-wall / party-wall exclusions depend on your traced exterior faces.
